    About

    I make things make sense. Whether that’s a dataset, a process, or a presentation — I find the clearest way to structure it and show it.

    My background

    I’m from Baia Mare, spent a year in Michigan on a scholarship, and studied Digital Media in Timișoara before heading back to the technical side with a master’s in Information Systems for Business. Along the way I’ve written technical docs at Nokia, tested software at Ikon Soft, mapped networks and public spending data at FabLab UVT, and now build the reporting behind publicly funded R&D projects at AUMOVIO. It’s a weird mix. It works.

    My approach

    I get uncomfortable when things are harder to understand than they need to be. That instinct drives most of what I do — whether I’m cleaning a dataset, mapping a network, building a dashboard, or designing a layout.
